Methodology

From a 240K-row file to 14,912 unique recall actions

A single NHTSA recall campaign can appear hundreds of times across models, model years, and manufacturers. We grouped by campaign number first, then ran every chart on the deduplicated table - the only way the counts represent real recall activity rather than data-shape artefacts.

Statistical evidence

Why every affected-unit chart uses a log scale

Not a stylistic choice. The raw distribution has skewness 23.86 - roughly 75x what a normal distribution allows. We applied log10 before plotting and tested the result.

Before / after log10 transformation

Both metrics drop to near-normal values. Shapiro-Wilk still rejects normality at n=5,000 (any tiny deviation becomes significant at that sample size), but the practical distribution is now usable.
